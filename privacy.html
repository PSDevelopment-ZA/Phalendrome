Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 9, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how PHALENDROME SOLUTIONS collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you contact us through our website contact form. This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company information
  • Project details or inquiry message

2. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your personal information to respond to your inquiries and fulfil our contractual obligations. By submitting the contact form, you consent to us using your information to contact you and manage your enquiry.

3. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry, fulfil our services, or meet legal obligations. If you request deletion, we will remove your personal data unless retention is required by law.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website uses Google Fonts for font delivery and may use third-party form handling services to process contact enquiries. Please review their privacy policies for more information.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

7. Your Rights

You may have rights regarding your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

8. Information Officer

The Information Officer for PHALENDROME SOLUTIONS can be contacted via email at info@phalendrome.co.za.

9. Lodging a Complaint

If you believe your personal information has been handled incorrectly, you may lodge a complaint with the South African Information Regulator at enquiries@inforegulator.org.za or visit inforegulator.org.za.

10. Contact

Email: info@phalendrome.co.za
